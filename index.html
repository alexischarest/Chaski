IN THE MAKING

Images that reach
a little further.

Profits from print sales will support Sayaka Inka, an Indigenous Andean family and musical group from Puno, Peru.

With ancestry rooted in the last Sayaka Inka village on the western shores of Lake Titicaca, this family of musicians continues sharing their culture with the modern world.

Available photographs, sizes, paper, pricing, and the details of this support will be shared before the shop opens.
